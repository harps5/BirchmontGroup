FAQ

Straight answers.

The questions owners ask us most. Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

What does it cost?

Our prices are published. A Kickstart Workshop is $1,250, an AI Opportunity Assessment is $2,500, Automation Sprints start at $7,500 and the AI Partner retainer starts at $1,500/month. Every project is fixed-price, so the number we quote is the number you pay.

We’re a small team. Is AI really worth it for us?

Usually, yes. Small teams often see the biggest relative gains because every hour recovered matters. If you’re unsure, the free scorecard or a 30-minute call will tell you quickly. If the return isn’t there, we’ll say so.

Do we have to start with the Assessment?

No. If you already know which workflow is costing you the most, we can scope an Automation Sprint directly. The Assessment is for businesses that want to know where to start, and its fee is credited toward your first Sprint.

Is our data safe?

We design around your data. We use business-grade AI services that don’t train on your information, give each system only the access it needs, keep sensitive data in Canada where required and document everything against PIPEDA and Alberta’s PIPA.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Rarely. Most of our work connects the tools you already pay for (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, QuickBooks, HubSpot and your industry software) and adds AI where the handoffs happen.

Will AI replace our staff?

Our focus is the repetitive work that burns people out, so they can spend more time with customers and on higher-value work. Most businesses use the recovered hours to grow without adding headcount.

Do you work outside Lethbridge?

Yes. We work on-site across Southern Alberta and remotely with businesses anywhere in Canada.
