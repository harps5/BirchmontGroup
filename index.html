FAQ

Straight answers.

The questions owners and operators ask us most. Don’t see yours? Ask us directly.

We’re a small team. Is AI really worth it for us?

Usually, yes — small teams often see the biggest relative gains because every hour recovered matters. Our assessment exists to answer this question with numbers before you commit to anything larger. If the return isn’t there, we’ll tell you.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped and quoted up front at a fixed fee, so there are no surprise invoices. Assessments and workshops are priced for small-business budgets, and implementation projects are sized to pay for themselves — typically within the first year.

Is our data safe?

We design around your data, not the other way around. We use business-grade AI services that don’t train on your information, apply least-privilege access, keep sensitive data in Canada where required, and document everything against PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy rules.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Rarely. Most of our work connects the tools you already pay for — Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, QuickBooks, HubSpot, your industry software — and adds AI where the handoffs happen.

Will AI replace our staff?

Our focus is removing the repetitive work that burns your people out, so they can spend more time with customers and on higher-value work. Most clients use the recovered hours to grow without adding headcount.

What happens after the project ends?

You own everything we build, with documentation and training so your team can run it. Many clients keep us on as a fractional AI partner for monitoring, improvements and new use cases — but there’s no lock-in.
