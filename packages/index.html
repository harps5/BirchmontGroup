Pricing FAQ

The fine print, in plain English.

Do we have to start with the Assessment?

No. If you already know which workflow is costing you the most, we’ll scope an Automation Sprint directly on a free call.

What isn’t included in the prices?

GST, and any software subscriptions your solution needs. Those are billed directly to you at cost, never marked up, and we’ll tell you what they are before you commit.

How is an Automation Sprint priced?

After a short scoping call we give you a fixed quote based on the number of systems involved and the complexity of the workflow. There’s no hourly billing and no change-order creep.

How does the Assessment credit work?

The full $2,500 Assessment fee is credited toward an Automation Sprint booked within 60 days of your roadmap readout.

What does the AI Partner retainer include?

Monitoring and maintenance of what we’ve built, a monthly block of improvement hours, quarterly roadmap reviews and priority support. There’s a three-month minimum, then it’s month-to-month.

How does the founding client program work?

Our first five clients get 20% off any package. In return, once your results are in, we ask for a short case study and an honest testimonial. Mention it when you get in touch.

Do you work on-site?

Yes. We work on-site across Southern Alberta and remotely with businesses anywhere in Canada. Workshops can run in person or by video.
