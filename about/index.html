Who We Are

A multi-division operating company built for small business.

Birchmont Group is a privately held operating company headquartered in Lethbridge, Alberta. We run three specialist divisions — Studio, Systems, and Advisory — each focused on serving small and medium businesses with work most agencies treat as too small to bother with.

We exist because the businesses that drive every Alberta town deserve better than templated websites, generic consulting, and software that wasn’t built for how they actually run. We deliver work at a standard usually reserved for enterprise clients — at a price and pace that fits a small business budget and timeline.

Our divisions share one operating discipline: clear scope, honest fit, recurring infrastructure over retainer creep. We’d rather pass on an engagement than sell something we wouldn’t buy ourselves.
