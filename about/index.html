Who We Are

Operators first, owners second.

Birchmont Group Inc. is a privately held operating company headquartered in Lethbridge, Alberta. We operate franchised restaurant locations, and we are building the commercial real estate platform that sits underneath them.

The two platforms are deliberately paired. Operating the restaurants tells us what a site is genuinely worth; owning the property removes the largest fixed cost a restaurant cannot otherwise control. Together they compound in a way neither would alone — cash flow from operations, equity from property, held on one balance sheet over a long horizon.

We are not a fund. There is no capital to deploy on a deadline, no investment committee, and no exit mandate. That lets us be patient on price, honest about fit, and willing to pass on a deal that only works on an aggressive case.

We are early and deliberately so. Our growth constraint is management depth, not capital appetite — we add a unit or an asset when the last one is stable and the team is deep enough to absorb the next.

Operating Principles

Four rules we
do not bend.

01

Underwrite the downside

Every unit and every building is modelled on a conservative case: realistic sales, current labour and interest rates, full occupancy cost. If it only clears on the optimistic case, we do not do it.

02

Own the ground where we can

Controlling the real estate under an operating business removes renewal risk, relocation risk, and rent escalation. Owning is the default; leasing is the exception we accept when the site is worth it.

03

Grow at the speed of the bench

Capital is rarely the binding constraint — capable managers are. We build the management bench ahead of the next location rather than opening and hoping we can staff it.

04

Hold for the long run

No flips, no forced dispositions, no fund clock. We buy assets and licences we expect to still own in twenty years, and we behave accordingly with tenants, franchisors, and staff.

“The best work is done quietly — with discipline, patience, and an unwavering commitment to fundamentals.”

Governance & Structure

How decisions
get made.

Birchmont Group Inc. is an Alberta corporation. Operating businesses and real property are held in separate subsidiaries so that each platform can be financed, insured, and governed on its own terms without exposing the other.

Acquisition and development decisions are made by the principals directly. Counterparties deal with the people who sign, which is why we can give a straight answer on a site or a listing in days rather than weeks.

Where we partner — joint ventures with local operators, sale-leasebacks, vendor financing — we document it properly and we honour it. Our reputation in a small number of markets is the only asset we cannot rebuild.
