About Birchmont

Operators first. Technologists second.

We started Birchmont because we kept seeing the same thing: powerful AI tools, and small businesses with no practical way to use them. We exist to close that gap.

Our story

Built from the operator’s side of the table.

Birchmont Group grew out of running real businesses, with payrolls to meet, customers to serve and never enough hours in the week. We know what it means when a process breaks on a Friday afternoon, and we know technology only matters if it makes Monday easier.

That’s the lens we bring to AI. We don’t sell software and we don’t chase trends. We look at how your business actually works, find the places where AI can take real weight off your team, and build solutions simple enough to trust and robust enough to rely on.

We publish our prices because that’s how we’d want to be treated. We start small because trust should be earned. And we measure everything in the currency owners care about: hours and dollars.

How we operate

What you can expect from us.

Honest advice

If AI isn’t the right answer to a problem, we’ll say so. Sometimes the best recommendation is a simpler process or a tool you already own.

Measured results

We agree on success metrics before we build, then report on hours saved, response times and cost impact after launch.

People-centred change

We design with your team, not around them. Adoption comes from tools that make people’s days better and training that builds confidence.

Responsible AI

Privacy, security and transparency are the defaults. Sensitive decisions keep a person in the loop, and your data is never used to train public models.

Where we work
