Who We Are

A multi-division enterprise built around operator credibility.

Birchmont Group Inc. is a privately held, Calgary-based operating company. We run three specialist divisions — Design Services, Consulting Services, and AI Services — each focused on serving small and medium businesses with the kind of work most agencies and consultancies treat as too small to bother with.

Every division is staffed by operators who’ve done the work themselves. Web designers who run their own sites. Consultants who’ve won the bids they help others write. AI practitioners who ship the workflows they recommend. No theory shops, no slide-ware, no junior staff billed at senior rates.

We’re built for the long run — honest scope, fixed price, transparent fit. We’d rather pass on an engagement than sell you something you don’t need.
