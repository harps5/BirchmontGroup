FAQ

Common questions.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on your needs. Engagements start as low as $1,000 upfront with a low monthly fee that covers hosting, maintenance, and edits. We’ll give you a fixed quote after the discovery call — no guessing, no hourly billing.

How long does a project take?

Most projects launch in three to four weeks from approval. Complex builds may take longer, but we’ll scope that clearly upfront.

Do I own the website?

Yes. You own your content and your domain. If you ever want to move on, we’ll hand over everything cleanly. No lock-in, no hostage situations.

What if I want to cancel?

Monthly hosting can be cancelled anytime with 30 days notice. We’ll export your site and help with the transition. We’d rather earn your business every month than trap you in a contract.

Do you do SEO or e-commerce?

Every site we build follows SEO best practices — fast load times, clean code, proper structure. For dedicated SEO campaigns or full e-commerce builds, we’ll discuss scope on the call and give you an honest answer about whether we’re the right fit.

Why don’t you list prices on the site?

Because every business is different. A five-page brochure site and a 20-page service business site need different things. We’d rather give you an accurate, tailored quote than a generic price list that doesn’t actually apply to you.
