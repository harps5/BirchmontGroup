FAQ

Common questions.

Is my business big enough for AI?

If you have repetitive work, scattered information, or a team spending hours on tasks a software process could do better — yes. Our methodology is built for SMBs specifically. The smaller you are, the more leverage a single well-placed AI workflow tends to deliver.

Do I need an in-house data team?

No. Most of our work uses the data you already have in tools you already pay for — CRM, email, docs, spreadsheets. We design for the team you have, not the team you don’t.

Will my data be safe?

Yes. We use enterprise-grade tools with proper data agreements, default to private/local processing where it matters, and document every integration. We’ll walk you through exactly where your data goes and what controls you have.

How long does an engagement take?

The Readiness Assessment runs about two weeks. Implementation typically runs four to eight weeks depending on scope. We’ll give you firm dates after the discovery call.

What if AI isn’t the right answer for us?

We’ll tell you. Sometimes the answer is a process change, a hire, or a different tool entirely. We’d rather pass on the engagement than sell you something you don’t need.

Do you build with ChatGPT, Claude, or something else?

We’re tool-agnostic. We’ve worked with the major model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google), automation platforms (Zapier, Make, n8n), and custom code where the off-the-shelf options don’t fit. We pick the right tool for your problem and your stack — not the one we’re paid to recommend.
