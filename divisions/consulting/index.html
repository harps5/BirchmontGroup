What We Do

We consult in the areas we operate.

Our consulting practice is built on a simple idea: the best advisors are the ones who’ve sat in your chair. Every specialist on our team has direct operational experience in their domain — not just frameworks and slide decks.

We work with businesses that need focused, practical guidance — whether you’re chasing a government contract, tightening restaurant operations, or trying to write a proposal that actually wins.

Our Specialties

Three areas. Deep expertise.

01

Government Contracting

Navigating procurement processes, compliance requirements, and vendor registration. We help businesses position themselves to compete for — and win — government work at the municipal, provincial, and federal level.

02

Bid & Proposal Writing

Proposals that get read and get scored. We help you craft clear, compliant, and compelling submissions — from RFP response strategy to final document assembly. No filler, no boilerplate.

03

QSR Operations

Unit economics, labour scheduling, food cost management, and multi-location playbooks. We advise QSR operators on the systems and processes that turn good restaurants into scalable ones.
