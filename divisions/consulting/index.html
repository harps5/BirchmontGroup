FAQ

Common questions.

How much does an engagement cost?

It depends on scope. A targeted bid review may be a few thousand dollars; a multi-month government contracting strategy is more. We always quote a fixed fee or a clearly capped hourly band — never an open-ended retainer unless that’s what you want.

How long does a typical engagement take?

Bid and proposal work is usually two to six weeks. Government contracting strategy and QSR operational engagements typically run one to three months. We’ll scope and date this clearly upfront.

Do you work outside Alberta or Canada?

Yes. Most engagements run remotely with periodic in-person work where it adds value. We work across Canada and selectively with US-based clients depending on the domain.

Can you guarantee we’ll win the bid?

No, and any consultant who does is selling you something. We’ll give you the strongest possible submission, sharp positioning, and honest feedback on probability of success — and we’ll tell you when a bid isn’t worth pursuing.

What industries do you serve outside QSR and government?

Bid and proposal writing transfers across most B2B and B2G sectors — we’ve worked across professional services, construction, technology, and trades. For deep operational consulting we focus on QSR specifically because that’s where our team has direct multi-location operating experience.
